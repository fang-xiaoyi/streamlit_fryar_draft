Cattle: key numbers for producers

September 2026 Cattle on Feed → Cattle October 2026 release

Bottom line. The Cattle October 2026 release USDA Cattle on Feed reported October 1 on-feed inventory of 11,372 thousand head, +204 thousand from the prior release's September 1 inventory. September placements were 1,777 thousand head (88% of the prior year), the lowest September placements in the series; September marketings were 1,524 thousand head (93% of prior year), and other disappearance was 49,000 thousand head (4% of prior year). Producer decision cue: the near-term inventory signal is not a standalone price forecast, but the low placement and marketing readings support watching future fed-cattle availability, basis, and timing of sales.

What it means for a producer

Decision cue. Use the low placement and marketing readings as an availability signal to monitor future fed-cattle supplies, basis, and sale timing—not as a standalone price forecast.

Three things to do next

  • Track placements and marketings together with basis and cash price discovery.
  • Compare the expected margin from a sale now versus a staged sale plan.
  • Use LRP or another protection strategy only after checking coverage, premium, and delivery timing.

Data and sources

Cattle on Feed is a monthly flow report rather than a WASDE vintage. The release-to-release comparison therefore compares the October 1 inventory and September flows with the September 1 inventory and August flows; year-over-year percentages remain the primary market read.
