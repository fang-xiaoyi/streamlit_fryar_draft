# Cattle USDA release report

**Current release:** Cattle October 2026 release  
**Previous release:** September 2026 Cattle on Feed  
**Current release date:** 2026-10-23  
**Previous release date:** 2026-09-18

The Cattle October 2026 release USDA Cattle on Feed reported October 1 on-feed inventory of 11,372 thousand head, +204 thousand from the prior release's September 1 inventory. September placements were 1,777 thousand head (88% of the prior year), the lowest September placements in the series; September marketings were 1,524 thousand head (93% of prior year), and other disappearance was 49,000 thousand head (4% of prior year). Producer decision cue: the near-term inventory signal is not a standalone price forecast, but the low placement and marketing readings support watching future fed-cattle availability, basis, and timing of sales.

## Data note
Cattle on Feed is a monthly flow report rather than a WASDE vintage. The release-to-release comparison therefore compares the October 1 inventory and September flows with the September 1 inventory and August flows; year-over-year percentages remain the primary market read. Snapshot 2026-10-23-revision-2 was validated by parser usda-refresh-1.0 and is retained as a release-specific vintage.
