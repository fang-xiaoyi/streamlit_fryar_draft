# Corn USDA release report

**Current release:** Corn October 2026  
**Previous release:** September 2026 WASDE  
**Current release date:** 2026-10-01  
**Previous release date:** 2026-09-11

Corn October 2026 USDA WASDE increased projected 2026/27 U.S. corn ending stocks from 1,567 to 1,849 million bushels, a +282 million bushel change (+18.0%). The revision reflects production rising +234 million bushels to 16,034 million bushels and total use rising +125 million bushels to 16,305 million bushels. Yield moved from 178.5 to 181.2 bushels per acre. The stocks-to-use ratio rose from 9.7% to 11.3% (+1.7 percentage points), while USDA's season-average farm-price projection moved from $4.80 to $4.70 per bushel. Producer decision cue: this is more bearish on stocks / more supportive of downside risk; review unpriced bushels, storage carry, and a staged hedge rather than treating the USDA number as a price forecast.

## Data note
The October-to-September comparison is a vintage comparison for the same 2026/27 marketing-year forecast. November 2026 WASDE was not yet released when this case was built. Snapshot 2026-10-01 was validated by parser usda-refresh-1.0 and is retained as a release-specific vintage.
