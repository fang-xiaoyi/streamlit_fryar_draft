# Corn USDA release report

**Current release:** Corn November 2026  
**Previous release:** October 2026 WASDE  
**Current release date:** 2026-11-01  
**Previous release date:** 2026-10-09

Corn November 2026 USDA WASDE increased projected 2026/27 U.S. corn ending stocks from 1,849 to 1,758 million bushels, a -91 million bushel change (-4.9%). The revision reflects production rising -141 million bushels to 15,893 million bushels and total use rising -50 million bushels to 16,255 million bushels. Yield moved from 181.2 to 179.6 bushels per acre. The stocks-to-use ratio rose from 11.3% to 10.8% (-0.5 percentage points), while USDA's season-average farm-price projection moved from $4.70 to $4.60 per bushel. Producer decision cue: this is more supportive on stocks / more constructive for price risk; review unpriced bushels, storage carry, and a staged hedge rather than treating the USDA number as a price forecast.

## Data note
The October-to-September comparison is a vintage comparison for the same 2026/27 marketing-year forecast. November 2026 WASDE was not yet released when this case was built. Snapshot 2026-11-01 was validated by parser usda-refresh-1.0 and is retained as a release-specific vintage.
